## Shared paper doctrine

- capability and execution evidence SHALL NOT be collapsed into authority
- each paper SHALL preserve its canonical family boundary
- cross-family dependencies SHALL be cited explicitly rather than merged conceptually
- formal claims SHALL remain traceable to canonical proofs, invariants, runtime behavior, or adversarial evidence
- publication prose SHALL NOT outrun repository-supported claims
- production deployment, regulatory approval, institutional adoption, external certification, customer validation, and legal sufficiency remain outside the canonical technical closure claim

## Standard paper structure

1. Abstract
2. Problem and failure mode
3. Canonical boundary
4. Formal model
5. Invariants and non-equivalence results
6. Proof obligations and counterexamples
7. Reference runtime
8. Adversarial verification
9. Cross-family interfaces
10. Limits and non-claims
11. Conclusion

## Publication sequence

The eight papers SHALL be drafted F1 through F8 so that later papers may cite earlier financial-family definitions while preserving independent family boundaries.
