## Frozen working title

**Ledger-State Finality, Record Integrity, and Reopenable Financial State**

This title is a boundary label for F4 development and is not yet a publication title.

## Frozen problem boundary

F4 addresses the boundary between financial finality as established in F3 and the ledger or record state that persists, represents, commits, reopens, or conflicts with that finality.

The supported problem is not merely whether an obligation is final. It is whether a ledger-state representation may validly encode, preserve, reopen, contradict, or reconstruct a financial state without collapsing record state into authority, historical identity, irreversibility, or continuity preservation.

## F4 begins where F3 leaves off

F3 established the distinction between:

- execution
- effect occurrence
- obligation satisfaction
- custody validity
- settlement completion
- finality
- irreversibility
- historical identity
- continuity preservation
- consequential closure

F4 inherits those distinctions and moves into the representation and persistence problem:

**final financial state != ledger representation of final financial state**

and therefore:

- ledger inclusion != authority
- ledger persistence != finality
- ledger equality != historical identity
- record commitment != irreversibility
- record restoration != continuity preservation
- record reopening != historical erasure
- state representation != valid transition
- recorded custody != custody authority

These are frozen as inheritance constraints only. Formal invariant numbering is deferred.
