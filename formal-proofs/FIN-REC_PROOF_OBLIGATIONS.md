## PO-FIN-REC-001 — Reconciliation Is Not Continuity

**Invariant:** FIN-REC-001

**Formal target:** Show that reconciliation, reconstruction, functional restoration, and preserved continuity remain non-equivalent and evidence-bound.

**Required premises:** Historical execution evidence; observed current state; remediation history; reconstruction/reconciliation evidence.

**Counterexample condition:** Reconciliation or reconstruction is classified as preserved continuity without sufficient continuity evidence.

**Evidence requirement:** Reconciliation/reconstruction inputs; historical evidence; divergence record; resulting classification evidence.

**Implementation requirement:** Reference runtime must keep reconciliation/reconstruction/continuity classifications distinct.

**Verification requirement:** Reconciliation/reconstruction/equivalence/continuity counterexamples.

**Dependencies:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Discharge state:** OPEN
