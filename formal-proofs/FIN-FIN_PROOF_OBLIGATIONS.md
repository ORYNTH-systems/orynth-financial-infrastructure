# FIN-FIN — Canonical Proof Obligations

## Status

All obligations in this file begin OPEN.

No obligation is proved or discharged by FIN-ARCH-06.

## PO-FIN-FIN-001 — Completion Is Not Finality

**Invariant:** FIN-FIN-001

**Formal target:** Show that finality is explicitly classified and remains distinct from execution completion, originating authority, historical validity, and continuity.

**Required premises:** Settlement history; finality model; governing conditions; evidence; reopening authority if applicable.

**Counterexample condition:** Finality is inferred solely from completion, elapsed time, inclusion, or balance state, or is treated as proof of continuity.

**Evidence requirement:** Finality-classification record; conditions; evidence basis; reopening evidence where applicable.

**Implementation requirement:** Reference runtime must expose explicit finality classification.

**Verification requirement:** Premature-finality, reopened-finality, and finality/continuity separation cases.

**Dependencies:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Discharge state:** OPEN
