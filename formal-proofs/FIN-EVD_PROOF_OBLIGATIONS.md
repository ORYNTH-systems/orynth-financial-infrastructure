# FIN-EVD — Canonical Proof Obligations

## Status

All obligations in this file begin OPEN.

No obligation is proved or discharged by FIN-ARCH-06.

---

## PO-FIN-EVD-001 — Execution Must Be Evidenced

**Invariant:** FIN-EVD-001

**Formal target:** Show that required financial execution and remediation outcomes remain reconstructably evidenced without evidence itself creating authority.

**Required premises:** Subject event/state; evidence requirements; provenance; integrity metadata; timing and lineage.

**Counterexample condition:** A governed execution/refusal/remediation cannot be reconstructed, or evidence is treated as authority.

**Evidence requirement:** Persistent provenance-linked evidence records sufficient for reconstruction and distinguishability.

**Implementation requirement:** Reference runtime must emit reconstructable evidence records for governed outcomes.

**Verification requirement:** Evidence loss, tampering, ambiguity, and reconstruction cases.

**Dependencies:** POB/UAA

**First major paper:** FOUNDATION

**Discharge state:** OPEN
