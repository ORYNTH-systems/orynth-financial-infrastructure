# FIN-CON — Canonical Proof Obligations

## Status

All obligations in this file begin OPEN.

No obligation is proved or discharged by FIN-ARCH-06.

---

## PO-FIN-CON-001 — Continuity Is Independently Classified

**Invariant:** FIN-CON-001

**Formal target:** Show that continuity classification is independent from reconstruction, restored function, finality, and later economic equivalence.

**Required premises:** Execution history; continuity-relevant evidence; reconstruction history; remediation history; classification basis.

**Counterexample condition:** Broken continuity is later classified preserved solely because function or balances were restored.

**Evidence requirement:** Continuity classification record; complete relevant execution/remediation history; evidence supporting preserved/broken/indeterminate status.

**Implementation requirement:** Reference runtime must expose explicit continuity classification.

**Verification requirement:** Broken/restored/reconstructed/indeterminate continuity cases.

**Dependencies:** EIP/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Discharge state:** OPEN
