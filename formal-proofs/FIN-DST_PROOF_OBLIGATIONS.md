# FIN-DST — Canonical Proof Obligations

## Status

All obligations in this file begin OPEN.

No obligation is proved or discharged by FIN-ARCH-06.

---

## PO-FIN-DST-001 — Consensus Is Not Authority

**Invariant:** FIN-DST-001

**Formal target:** Show that distributed consensus, ledger validity, or one participant's authority cannot independently establish whole-chain governance authority.

**Required premises:** Participant identities; authority references; remote state; consensus or ledger state; applicable execution boundaries.

**Counterexample condition:** Consensus, ledger validity, or a single participant's authorization is treated as sufficient whole-chain authority.

**Evidence requirement:** Participant authority records; remote-state observations; consensus/ledger evidence; cross-boundary execution trace.

**Implementation requirement:** Distributed adapters must preserve authority/admission separation from consensus/ledger validity.

**Verification requirement:** Consensus-without-authority, multi-party authority conflict, remote-state drift cases.

**Dependencies:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F4

**Discharge state:** OPEN
