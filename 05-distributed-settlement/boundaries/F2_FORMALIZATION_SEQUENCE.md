## Sequencing Rule

No consensus mathematics SHALL be introduced before FIN-F2-02 defines
participant identity, authority ownership, scope, and required actor sets.

No finality theorem SHALL be introduced before FIN-F2-06.

No runtime result SHALL be represented as formal proof.

No F1 theorem or semantic definition SHALL be rewritten by F2.
