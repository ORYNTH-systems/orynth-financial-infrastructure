# DEI Binding

## Publication

Deterministic Execution Integrity for Autonomous Financial Infrastructure

## Canonical Contribution

DEI provides the initial unified formalization of financially consequential execution integrity across authorization, verification, orchestration, execution, settlement, and audit.

## F1-F8 Relationship

F1-F8 are extensions beyond DEI.

Each future paper SHALL identify:

- inherited DEI definitions,
- preserved assumptions,
- removed assumptions,
- extended state space,
- new theorem or proposition,
- new proof obligations,
- new runtime mechanisms,
- new verification surface.

## Non-Redefinition Rule

No F1-F8 paper may present an inherited DEI primitive as a new contribution.
