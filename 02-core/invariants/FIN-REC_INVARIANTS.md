# FIN-REC — Reconciliation and Reconstruction Invariants

## Purpose

Separate reconciliation, reconstruction, economic equivalence, and restoration.

## Canonical Status

These invariants are architecturally asserted by FIN-ARCH-05.

Assertion does not constitute proof.

FIN-ARCH-06 SHALL assign explicit proof obligations.

---

## FIN-REC-001 — Reconciliation Is Not Continuity

**Normative rule:** RECONCILED SHALL NOT imply continuity was preserved.

**Applies to:** Reconciliation

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REC-002 — Reconstruction Is Not Restoration

**Normative rule:** Reconstructed historical state SHALL NOT be classified as restored continuity merely because historical state can be reconstructed.

**Applies to:** Reconstruction

**Inherited / derived from:** POB/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REC-003 — Functional Restoration Is Not Historical Restoration

**Normative rule:** Restored economic function SHALL NOT imply restored historical integrity.

**Applies to:** Recovery

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REC-004 — Reconciliation Must Preserve Divergence

**Normative rule:** Observed divergence among historical execution effect settlement and current state SHALL remain representable after reconciliation.

**Applies to:** Reconciliation

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REC-005 — Unknown History Cannot Be Declared Restored

**Normative rule:** Where required historical evidence is insufficient the architecture SHALL classify reconstruction or continuity as indeterminate rather than restored.

**Applies to:** Reconstruction

**Inherited / derived from:** POB

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED
