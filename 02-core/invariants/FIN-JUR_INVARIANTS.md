# FIN-JUR — Jurisdiction Invariants

## Purpose

Require current jurisdictional applicability and prevent authority expansion across governance boundaries.

## Canonical Status

These invariants are architecturally asserted by FIN-ARCH-05.

Assertion does not constitute proof.

FIN-ARCH-06 SHALL assign explicit proof obligations.

---

## FIN-JUR-001 — Jurisdiction Is Execution Relevant State

**Normative rule:** Applicable jurisdiction SHALL be treated as execution-relevant state where it affects authority scope or admissibility.

**Applies to:** Cross-jurisdiction execution

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F7

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-JUR-002 — Initial Jurisdiction Does Not Permanently Govern

**Normative rule:** Jurisdiction applicable at authorization SHALL NOT be presumed unchanged at later execution boundaries.

**Applies to:** Cross-border execution

**Inherited / derived from:** DEI/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F7

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-JUR-003 — Cross-Boundary Authority Cannot Expand Implicitly

**Normative rule:** Crossing an institutional geographic regulatory or ledger boundary SHALL NOT implicitly expand authority.

**Applies to:** Cross-boundary execution

**Inherited / derived from:** UAA/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F7

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-JUR-004 — Jurisdiction Conflict Must Be Explicit

**Normative rule:** Conflicting applicable jurisdictional constraints SHALL remain explicitly representable and SHALL NOT be silently collapsed.

**Applies to:** Jurisdiction conflict

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F7

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-JUR-005 — Indeterminate Jurisdiction Cannot Authorize

**Normative rule:** Where required jurisdictional applicability cannot be established the affected consequential transition SHALL NOT be admitted.

**Applies to:** Jurisdiction-sensitive transitions

**Inherited / derived from:** UAA/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F7

**Status:** ASSERTED

---
