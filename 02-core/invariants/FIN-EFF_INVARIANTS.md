# FIN-EFF — Economic Effect Invariants

## Purpose

Distinguish no effect, partial effect, completed effect, compensation, and reversal.

## Canonical Status

These invariants are architecturally asserted by FIN-ARCH-05.

Assertion does not constitute proof.

FIN-ARCH-06 SHALL assign explicit proof obligations.

---

## FIN-EFF-001 — No Effect Is Distinct From Partial Effect

**Normative rule:** NO_EFFECT and PARTIAL_EFFECT SHALL remain distinct observable states.

**Applies to:** Post-execution effect classification

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EFF-002 — Partial Effect Must Be Observable

**Normative rule:** Any detected subset of intended consequential economic mutation SHALL be representable as PARTIAL_EFFECT.

**Applies to:** Post-effect execution

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EFF-003 — Effect Does Not Establish Legitimacy

**Normative rule:** Occurrence of economic effect SHALL NOT establish that the originating execution was authorized admissible or governance-valid.

**Applies to:** All economic effects

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EFF-004 — Later Effect Does Not Erase Earlier Effect

**Normative rule:** Compensation reversal or later economic equivalence SHALL NOT erase evidence of an earlier effect.

**Applies to:** Post-effect resolution

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1/F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EFF-005 — Indeterminate Effect Is Not No Effect

**Normative rule:** Insufficient evidence of effect SHALL be classified as INDETERMINATE_EFFECT rather than NO_EFFECT.

**Applies to:** Effect classification

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED
