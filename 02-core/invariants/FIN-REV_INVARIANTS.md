# FIN-REV — Reversal Invariants

## Purpose

Govern reversal as a distinct consequential execution while preserving original history.

## Canonical Status

These invariants are architecturally asserted by FIN-ARCH-05.

Assertion does not constitute proof.

FIN-ARCH-06 SHALL assign explicit proof obligations.

---

## FIN-REV-001 — Reversal Is New Execution

**Normative rule:** A reversal SHALL be represented as a new financially consequential execution event.

**Applies to:** Reversal

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REV-002 — Reversal Requirement Is Not Reversal Authority

**Normative rule:** REVERSAL_REQUIRED SHALL NOT imply authority or admission to enter REVERSING.

**Applies to:** Reversal lifecycle

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization/UAA

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REV-003 — Reversal Must Be Independently Admitted

**Normative rule:** Reversal SHALL pass an execution admission boundary independently of the originating execution.

**Applies to:** Reversal

**Inherited / derived from:** UAA/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REV-004 — Reversal Does Not Erase History

**Normative rule:** REVERSED SHALL NOT imply that the original execution or economic effect never occurred.

**Applies to:** Historical state

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1/F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-REV-005 — State Equivalence After Reversal Is Not Historical Equivalence

**Normative rule:** Equivalent economic values after reversal SHALL NOT establish equivalence of historical financial state.

**Applies to:** Reversal and reconstruction

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED
