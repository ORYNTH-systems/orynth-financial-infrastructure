# FIN-DST — Distributed Execution Invariants

## Purpose

Prevent consensus or replicated agreement from becoming implicit financial authority.

## Canonical Status

These invariants are architecturally asserted by FIN-ARCH-05.

Assertion does not constitute proof.

FIN-ARCH-06 SHALL assign explicit proof obligations.

---

## FIN-DST-001 — Consensus Is Not Authority

**Normative rule:** Distributed consensus SHALL NOT by itself establish financial execution authority.

**Applies to:** Distributed ledgers and multi-party systems

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F4

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-DST-002 — Ledger Validity Is Not Governance Admission

**Normative rule:** A ledger-valid transaction SHALL NOT be presumed governance-admissible.

**Applies to:** Blockchain and distributed ledgers

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F4

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-DST-003 — Multi-Party Execution Requires Composable Authority

**Normative rule:** A multi-party financial transition SHALL NOT infer whole-chain authority from the authority of any single participant.

**Applies to:** Clearing settlement and distributed finance

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F2

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-DST-004 — Remote State Is Execution Relevant

**Normative rule:** Execution-relevant remote counterparty or ledger state SHALL be incorporated where its change can alter admissibility.

**Applies to:** Distributed execution

**Inherited / derived from:** AOMS/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F2/F4

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-DST-005 — Distributed Finality Does Not Erase Governance Failure

**Normative rule:** Consensus finality or replicated finality SHALL NOT erase evidence of prior authority admission or continuity failure.

**Applies to:** Distributed finality

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F4

**Status:** ASSERTED

---
