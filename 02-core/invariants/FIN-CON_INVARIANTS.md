# FIN-CON — Continuity Invariants

## Purpose

Preserve distinction between continuous integrity, reconstruction, restored function, and reconciled history.

## Canonical Status

These invariants are architecturally asserted by FIN-ARCH-05.

Assertion does not constitute proof.

FIN-ARCH-06 SHALL assign explicit proof obligations.

---

## FIN-CON-001 — Continuity Is Independently Classified

**Normative rule:** Financial continuity SHALL be classified independently from execution completion settlement finality and current balance.

**Applies to:** Continuity

**Inherited / derived from:** EIP/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-CON-002 — Reconstruction Does Not Restore Continuity

**Normative rule:** RECONSTRUCTED SHALL NOT imply PRESERVED continuity.

**Applies to:** Continuity

**Inherited / derived from:** POB/EIP

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-CON-003 — Restored Function Does Not Restore Continuity

**Normative rule:** FUNCTIONALLY_RESTORED SHALL NOT imply PRESERVED continuity.

**Applies to:** Recovery

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-CON-004 — Continuity Break Survives Later Equivalence

**Normative rule:** A demonstrated continuity break SHALL remain historically true even if later state becomes economically equivalent.

**Applies to:** Continuity history

**Inherited / derived from:** Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-CON-005 — Indeterminate Continuity Is Not Preserved Continuity

**Normative rule:** Insufficient continuity evidence SHALL result in INDETERMINATE rather than PRESERVED classification.

**Applies to:** Continuity

**Inherited / derived from:** EIP/POB

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED
