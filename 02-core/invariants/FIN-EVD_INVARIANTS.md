# FIN-EVD — Evidence Invariants

## Purpose

Require persistent evidence for execution, refusal, effect, remediation, classification, and historical lineage.

## Canonical Status

These invariants are architecturally asserted by FIN-ARCH-05.

Assertion does not constitute proof.

FIN-ARCH-06 SHALL assign explicit proof obligations.

---

## FIN-EVD-001 — Execution Must Be Evidenced

**Normative rule:** Consequential execution attempts and outcomes SHALL produce reconstructable execution evidence.

**Applies to:** Execution

**Inherited / derived from:** POB/UAA

**First major paper:** FOUNDATION

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EVD-002 — Governed Refusal Must Be Distinguishable

**Normative rule:** A governed financial refusal SHALL remain distinguishable from absence crash timeout or unobserved execution.

**Applies to:** Blocked execution

**Inherited / derived from:** POB/DEI

**First major paper:** FOUNDATION

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EVD-003 — Evidence Does Not Create Authority

**Normative rule:** Evidence telemetry measurement or observation SHALL NOT create financial execution authority.

**Applies to:** All evidence

**Inherited / derived from:** UAA

**First major paper:** FOUNDATION

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EVD-004 — Remediation Must Preserve Lineage

**Normative rule:** Compensation reversal and reconciliation SHALL preserve evidence lineage to the originating execution.

**Applies to:** Post-effect remediation

**Inherited / derived from:** POB/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F1

**Status:** ASSERTED

---

## FIN-EVD-005 — Classification Must Be Evidence Backed

**Normative rule:** Finality continuity and reconstruction classifications SHALL reference sufficient evidence.

**Applies to:** Classification

**Inherited / derived from:** POB/Financial specialization

**First major paper:** F3

**Status:** ASSERTED

---
