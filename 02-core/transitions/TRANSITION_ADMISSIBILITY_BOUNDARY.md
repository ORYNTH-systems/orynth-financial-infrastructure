# Transition Admissibility Boundary

## Purpose

This document prevents confusion between structural transition existence and execution admissibility.

A transition may be:

1. structurally defined,
2. currently inadmissible,
3. admitted,
4. attempted,
5. executed,
6. evidenced.

These are distinct facts.

---

## Required Transition Questions

For every financially consequential transition:

1. Is the transition defined?
2. Who or what is the actor?
3. What authority reference applies?
4. Is authority current?
5. Is scope valid?
6. Are governing constraints satisfied?
7. Is the relevant state current?
8. Is the transition admitted?
9. Was execution attempted?
10. Did economic effect occur?
11. What evidence survives?
12. What settlement state resulted?
13. What finality classification applies?
14. What continuity classification applies?

---

## Compensation / Reversal Rule

A requirement for compensation or reversal does not itself authorize that action.

COMPENSATION_REQUIRED != COMPENSATING

REVERSAL_REQUIRED != REVERSING

A new execution admission boundary must exist before consequential compensating or reversal effect.
