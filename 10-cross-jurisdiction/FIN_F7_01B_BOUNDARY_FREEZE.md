# FIN-F7-01B — Cross-Jurisdiction Financial Execution Boundary Freeze

Status: COMPLETE — PASS

## Canonical family

Cross-Jurisdiction Financial Execution

## Frozen problem boundary

F7 governs the distinction between technical or operational financial reach across jurisdictions and the jurisdictional authority required to admit consequential financial execution.

The primary F7 question is not whether a system can technically reach, route, settle, custody, invoke, transfer, or record a financial action across borders or jurisdictional boundaries.

The primary question is whether the requested financial action is supported by current, applicable, scoped, jurisdictionally valid authority for every consequential boundary it crosses.

## Frozen core separation

technical reach != jurisdictional authority

Technical accessibility, network reach, cross-border routing capability, asset presence, custody location, settlement venue, counterparty location, regulatory applicability, or execution capability SHALL NOT by itself constitute jurisdictional financial authority.

## Frozen jurisdictional authority boundary

Jurisdictional financial authority MUST be attributable to a valid external or previously admitted authority source applicable to the requested financial operation.

Authority valid in one jurisdiction MUST NOT be presumed valid in another jurisdiction without explicit jurisdictional applicability.

Where multiple jurisdictions are consequentially implicated, F7 MUST preserve each jurisdictional authority boundary independently.

Jurisdictional conflict MUST remain explicit. Conflict SHALL NOT be resolved by selecting the broadest, easiest, most technically reachable, or most permissive authority.

Delegated cross-jurisdiction authority MUST remain source-bound, scoped, temporally valid, jurisdictionally applicable, and attenuated relative to its parent authority.

## Frozen multi-jurisdiction rule

Technical ability to perform a single financial operation across multiple jurisdictions SHALL NOT collapse those jurisdictions into one authority domain.

A consequential cross-jurisdiction operation MUST preserve distinguishable authority applicability for each required jurisdictional boundary.

## Cross-family inheritance

F7 inherits F6 programmable-finance authority boundaries without treating executable code, automation, oracle input, or contract state as jurisdictional authority.

F7 inherits F5 cryptographic-authority boundaries without treating key possession, signature validity, wallet control, or custody capability as jurisdictional authority.

F7 inherits F4 ledger-state distinctions without treating ledger location or representation as constitutive jurisdictional truth.

F7 inherits F2 consensus distinctions without treating network acceptance as jurisdictional authorization.

F7 leaves autonomous economic principal and machine financial authority to F8.

## Freeze control

The F7 problem boundary is frozen.

Canonical objects, state domains, predicates, decisions, transitions, invariants, proof obligations, runtime components, tests, and publication surfaces remain unfrozen.
