# F1 — Scope and Non-Claims

## In Scope

F1 addresses:

- financially consequential execution after economic effect has begun,
- partial settlement,
- interrupted economic mutation,
- post-effect containment,
- compensation,
- reversal,
- reconciliation,
- historical preservation,
- initial economic continuity classification.

---

## Out of Scope

F1 does not attempt to solve:

- generic authority origination,
- generic identity continuity,
- general policy reconciliation,
- distributed consensus,
- blockchain execution,
- digital-asset custody,
- smart-contract semantics,
- jurisdictional conflict,
- autonomous-agent principal resolution,
- full multi-party clearing,
- complete financial finality theory.

Those belong to inherited systems or later papers F2-F8.

---

## Non-Claims

F1 does not claim:

- compensation restores original history,
- reversal erases original execution,
- balance restoration restores continuity,
- settlement completion proves lawful authority,
- technical success proves governance validity,
- all financial systems support deterministic reversal,
- all economic effects are reversible,
- all partial settlement states can be compensated,
- legal finality is identical to technical finality.

---

## Safety Boundary

Where post-effect state is indeterminate, F1 must preserve indeterminacy.

It must not falsely convert uncertainty into:

NO_EFFECT,
COMPLETED,
REVERSED,
RESTORED,
or CONTINUITY_PRESERVED.
