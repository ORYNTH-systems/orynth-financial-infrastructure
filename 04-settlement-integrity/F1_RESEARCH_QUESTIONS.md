# F1 — Canonical Research Questions

## RQ-1

How must economic effect be represented when execution is neither zero-effect nor fully completed?

## RQ-2

What evidence is sufficient to distinguish:

NO_EFFECT,
PARTIAL_EFFECT,
COMPLETED_EFFECT,
and INDETERMINATE_EFFECT?

## RQ-3

What is the minimum valid containment action after partial effect?

## RQ-4

Under what conditions should compensation be required?

## RQ-5

Why must compensation be modeled as a new execution rather than as implicit rollback?

## RQ-6

Under what conditions should reversal be required?

## RQ-7

What formally distinguishes compensation from reversal?

## RQ-8

Can a compensating or reversal execution itself partially fail?

## RQ-9

How should recursively partial remediation be represented?

## RQ-10

What constitutes sufficient reconciliation after partial, compensated, or reversed financial execution?

## RQ-11

When can two resulting financial states be economically equivalent but historically non-equivalent?

## RQ-12

What evidence must survive remediation?

## RQ-13

When can continuity be classified preserved, broken, reconstructed, restored-function-only, or indeterminate?

## RQ-14

What classes of financial effect are inherently non-reversible?

## RQ-15

What properties are necessary before post-effect state may be considered stable enough to leave active remediation?
