# F1 — Removed or Relaxed Assumptions

F1 deliberately removes several simplifying assumptions used by the pre-effect model.

## RA-1 — No-Effect Assumption Removed

F1 does not assume invalid execution is always detected before economic mutation.

---

## RA-2 — Atomic Effect Assumption Removed

F1 does not assume financial execution produces either full effect or zero effect.

Partial economic effect is explicitly representable.

---

## RA-3 — Automatic Rollback Assumption Rejected

F1 does not assume a prior state can always be restored through technical rollback.

---

## RA-4 — Compensation Equals Reversal Assumption Rejected

Compensation and reversal remain distinct execution classes.

---

## RA-5 — Balance Restoration Equals State Restoration Assumption Rejected

Equivalent balances do not establish equivalent history.

---

## RA-6 — Completion Equals Finality Assumption Rejected

Execution completion and settlement finality remain distinct.

---

## RA-7 — Remediation Inherits Authority Assumption Rejected

Compensation and reversal require independent authority and admission.

---

## RA-8 — Reconciliation Equals Continuity Assumption Rejected

A reconciled state may still reflect broken continuity.
