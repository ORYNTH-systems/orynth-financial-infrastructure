# F1 — Inherited Assumptions

F1 inherits the following baseline properties.

## IA-1

Capability does not imply authority.

## IA-2

Prior authorization does not imply current admission.

## IA-3

Consequential execution requires current admission.

## IA-4

Execution identity remains bound to actor, action, rule, scope, and applicable version.

## IA-5

Replay does not recreate authority.

## IA-6

Evidence does not create authority.

## IA-7

Governed refusal before economic mutation may produce deterministic no-effect semantics.

## IA-8

Settlement state is distinct from execution state.

## IA-9

Finality is distinct from continuity.

## IA-10

Historical evidence must survive later remediation.

These assumptions are inherited.

F1 may specialize them but may not silently weaken or redefine them.
