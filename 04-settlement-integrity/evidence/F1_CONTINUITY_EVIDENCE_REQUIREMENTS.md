# F1 Economic Continuity Evidence Requirements

A continuity classification must be based on the relevant execution path, not only
the terminal economic state.

Minimum evidence requirements include:

1. continuity profile identifier;
2. declared continuity requirement set Gamma;
3. relevant execution history;
4. requirement-state trace for each gamma;
5. timing of degradation or qualifying break;
6. compensation/reversal events where relevant;
7. reconciliation result;
8. reconstruction event where applicable;
9. current continuity state;
10. historical break record;
11. provenance sufficient to support each classification.

For PRESERVED:

Evidence must establish the absence of qualifying breaks across the complete
relevant interval.

Absence of known break evidence alone is insufficient.

For DEGRADED:

Evidence must establish a bounded impairment that does not meet the profile's
break threshold.

For BROKEN:

Evidence must establish at least one qualifying continuity violation.

For RECONSTRUCTED:

Evidence must establish:

break
-> reconstruction process
-> newly accepted continuity relation.

The break record SHALL remain preserved.

For INDETERMINATE:

Evidence must identify the material unresolved condition preventing unique
classification.

A conformant record must never rewrite:

BROKEN -> PRESERVED

merely because economic state was later repaired.
